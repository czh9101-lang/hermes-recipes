# Hermes Wingtips #99: copy the agent's last answer

posted 2026-10-12. original: [https://x.com/witcheer/status/2109554664605725085](https://x.com/witcheer/status/2109554664605725085)

want an answer from your Hermes Agent in an email or a doc?

`/copy`

it puts the agent's last answer on your clipboard, ready to paste. type it in the terminal chat, right after the answer you want.

handy when your agent drafts something you're about to send!

docs: [https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/reference/slash-commands](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/reference/slash-commands)
