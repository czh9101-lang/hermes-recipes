# Hermes Wingtips #97: let your agent ask before it starts

posted 2026-10-11. original: [https://x.com/witcheer/status/2109184368052777380](https://x.com/witcheer/status/2109184368052777380)

want your Hermes Agent to check the details with you?

"ask me a few questions first"

add that to your message. in the terminal, it can put its questions together in one panel, with choices to pick or room for your own answer.
